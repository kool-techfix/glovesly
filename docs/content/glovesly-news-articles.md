## 1. Glovesly named in two preferred-bidder consortia for Nigeria's aviation leasing and MRO plans

**Slug:** glovesly-preferred-bidder-aviation-leasing-and-mro
**Reported on:** 12 January 2021
**Tags:** Aviation, MRO, Public-private partnership

**Excerpt:**
The Federal Ministry of Aviation named Glovesly Pro-Project Limited as a partner in two consortia chosen as preferred bidders for a national Aviation Leasing Company and a Maintenance, Repair and Overhaul (MRO) centre in Abuja.

**Body:**
The Federal Ministry of Aviation has announced the preferred bidders for two aviation projects after evaluating the proposals it received in response to its Requests for Proposals.

For the Aviation Leasing Company (ALC), the ministry selected a consortium of A J Walter Leasing Limited and Glovesly Pro-Project Limited. For the MRO centre, it selected a consortium of A J Walter Aviation Limited, EgyptAir Maintenance & Engineering (EGME) and Glovesly Pro-Project Limited.

The ALC is planned as a joint venture between the Federal Government and a private investor. In the short term it would lease aircraft from international lessors and sub-lease them to African airlines, with a longer-term aim of buying and leasing its own aircraft to carriers on the continent.

The MRO facility is to be built at the Nnamdi Azikiwe International Airport in Abuja under a public-private partnership on a build, operate and transfer basis. It is designed to service narrow-body (jet and turboprop) and wide-body aircraft.

Next steps set out by the ministry included a due diligence visit to the bidders' premises, in line with Infrastructure Concession Regulatory Commission (ICRC) guidelines, followed by negotiations, approval of a Full Business Case by the ICRC and the Federal Executive Council, and the signing of agreements. Implementation was expected to start in the third quarter of 2021.

*This article reports the position as announced in January 2021.*

## 2. Federal Executive Council approves N1.12 billion civil works contract for Glovesly on Abuja aircraft maintenance centre

**Slug:** fec-approves-civil-works-contract-abuja-aircraft-maintenance-centre
**Reported on:** 13 October 2022 (council meeting held on Wednesday, 12 October 2022)
**Tags:** Aviation, Federal Executive Council, Abuja, Civil works

**Excerpt:**
The Federal Executive Council approved the civil works for an aircraft maintenance centre in Abuja, awarding them to Glovesly Pro-Project at N1.12 billion with a 12-month completion period.

**Body:**
The Federal Executive Council (FEC) has approved contracts for an Aircraft Maintenance Centre in Abuja as part of a wider approval of N225.2 billion for projects and procurements across the ministries of Aviation, Police Affairs, Works, and Housing and Health.

The Minister of Aviation, Senator Hadi Sirika, briefed journalists after the council meeting at the Presidential Villa, Abuja. He said the building structure contract was awarded to Ingenious Nigeria Limited at N3.98 billion, while the civil works were awarded to Glovesly Pro-Project at N1.12 billion, to be completed in 12 months.

The minister explained that the centre belongs to the government's plan for a maintenance, repair and overhaul facility in Nigeria. He said it would let aircraft be serviced in the country instead of being flown abroad, which would keep skilled labour and foreign exchange at home and support the transfer of technology.

The same meeting approved N7.9 billion for contracts to set up a Fire Truck Maintenance and Refurbishment Centre in Katsina.

## 3. Expanded General Aviation Terminal in Lagos inaugurated, with Glovesly as contractor

**Slug:** expanded-general-aviation-terminal-lagos-commissioned
**Event date:** Monday, 22 May 2023
**Tags:** Aviation, Terminal buildings, Lagos, FAAN

**Excerpt:**
The Minister of Aviation and Aerospace inaugurated the first phase of the expanded General Aviation Terminal at the Lagos airport, a N2.2 billion project executed by Glovesly Pro-Project Limited that raised the terminal's capacity from 254 to 1,024 passengers.

**Body:**
The Minister of Aviation and Aerospace, Senator Hadi Sirika, has inaugurated the first phase of the expanded General Aviation Terminal (GAT) at the Lagos airport. The project cost N2.2 billion and was executed by Glovesly Pro-Project Limited. It raises the terminal's capacity from 254 passengers to 1,024.

At the inauguration on Monday, 22 May 2023, the minister said the work was started to deal with a long-standing shortage of space. Passenger numbers had grown beyond what the facilities could handle, especially at peak periods, and the Zulu terminal built earlier to ease the pressure had not fully solved the problem. He described the expansion as a short-term measure and said the ministry intends to build a much larger domestic terminal in future.

The expanded terminal has new protocol lounges, airline office space and VIP lounges, along with a central air-conditioning unit, a fire alarm and sprinkler system and an aviation mini clinic.

The minister praised the contractor for completing the first phase on time, noting that the work began early in 2022. He said it is only the first phase and expressed the hope that the next administration would carry on with the second phase. He also said he expected the facility to improve passenger handling at the terminal and to raise revenue for the Federal Airports Authority of Nigeria (FAAN).

**Source:** Punch, "Sirika inaugurates N2bn expanded GAT in Lagos airport".
https://punchng.com/sirika-inaugurates-n2bn-expanded-gat-in-lagos-airport/
